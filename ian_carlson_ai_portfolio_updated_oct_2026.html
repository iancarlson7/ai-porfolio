About

I’m building my career at the intersection of finance, technology, and AI.

I want to be the person who understands both—the financial decision in front of me and the technology changing how that decision gets made.

I’m Ian Carlson, a Finance and Information Systems / Business Analytics student at Iona University with a minor focus in Artificial Intelligence. The easiest way to describe me is that I’m always trying to understand how things work—and then figure out how to use that understanding to make a better decision.

Finance was my starting point. I actively follow the market, research companies, manage my own investment portfolio, and spend a lot of time thinking about valuation, risk, growth, and where technology is creating the next major opportunity. That naturally pulled me deeper into analytics, information systems, and AI.

AI is already part of how I work every day. I use it to pressure-test ideas, organize information, improve research, explore data, build projects, and turn rough thoughts into something useful. I’m interested in the point where AI stops being a cool demo and starts becoming an actual advantage in business or finance.

Outside the classroom, I like being in positions where I have real responsibility. I serve as Vice President of Finance for Iona’s Student Government Association, help lead the Information Systems Club as Vice President, work with prospective students as an Admissions Tour Guide, and stay involved in campus life. Those roles have pushed me to communicate clearly, make decisions, manage responsibility, and work with a lot of different people.

This portfolio is a record of that development. I’m not trying to make every project look perfect. I want it to show how I think, what I build, what I learn, and how my ability to use AI improves over time.

A little more about me
I like having skin in the game. Following markets and investing my own money makes finance real to me. Decisions have consequences, and that forces me to keep learning.
I’m naturally competitive. I’m always looking for the extra information, tool, or perspective that can help me understand something better than I did yesterday.
I learn by building. I understand technology best when I can apply it to a real problem, test it, break it, improve it, and see what actually works.
I want range. My goal is to be comfortable in a financial model, a dataset, a business conversation, or an AI workflow—and understand how they connect.
I’m thinking long term. I’m building skills now that can compound into a career at the intersection of finance, technology, analytics, and intelligent systems.
